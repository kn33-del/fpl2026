     `target_ff_count`, explicit `ranges`, `place_directive`,
     `route_directive` (see `action_parameters_schema` in the timing context).
   - Prefer this over the clustering `pblock` action when many paths fail
     with high spread, or when clustering pblocks have stopped helping.

3. **Clustering Pblock — `pblock`** (small region around critical-path cells):
   - The orchestrator automatically sizes the region per candidate path, runs
     fabric analysis, range conversion, pblock creation, re-placement, and
     re-routing. A region that overlaps a previously applied pblock is
     resolved automatically (the stale pblock is deleted), so overlap
     failures no longer recur. You can still override sizing with
     `target_lut_count`/`target_ff_count` in `action_parameters`.
   - For 1-2 paths with spread, try phys_opt_design first instead

4. **Physical Optimization** (Post-place/route fine-tuning):
   - Use phys_opt_design with appropriate directives
   - Good for 1-2 problematic paths
   - Can be combined with other strategies
